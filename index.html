MA Chidambaram Stadium, Chepauk: A Game-changing Track

Chennai's MA Chidambaram Stadium is a unique cricket ground - a memorable venue with history and character, and a record for delivering exciting tactical cricket that is as much about the pitch as it is about the players. Historically, Chepauk has favoured spinners, with a pitch that grips and turns and punish batters who do not get their players' craft early in the innings. This year, Chepauk has revealed a more batsman-friendly aspect with three of four innings surpassing 200 runs. The match's outcome is likely to depend heavily on the toss, with fingers likely to be crossed by both captains for a chase in the final, given the conditions' favouring of the second team. An expected evening temperature of 31°C with 67% humidity means that it will be a warm, humid night where fast seamers who bowl at good length, and spinners who bowl at different speeds, will thrive.

CSK have never lost a game at home here, totalling an incredible 17 wins without loss, but the Mumbai bowlers have struggled against the quality of CSK's bowling in recent years and both sides' batting strength has contributed to a truer pitch (relative to other Chennai venues) than the ground's reputation for spin would suggest this season.
